# Smartphone gameplay visual direction

## Cutout market

Grumpy Town uses an elevated three-quarter camera and original paper-cutout
characters. The design brief follows a teal-hooded player, a rival sliding from
water onto ice, and a furious guard losing their footing. The market continues
beyond the screen. Floating movement and Dash + Drop controls fit in the bottom
quarter.

Use the [cutout brief](cutout-market-prompt.md) for the original art direction,
the [Babylon construction guide](../babylon-scene-construction.md) for geometry
and animation, and [mobile controls](../mobile-and-controls.md) for responsive
dimensions. The [local trial](../babylon-local-trial.md) describes the playable
scene and how to try it.

## Earlier designs

Paper Market Caper explored paper textures, Pocket Arcade Market used top-down
pixel art, and Toybox Heist used toy-like sprites. The early Phaser prototype
used a square playfield with a dedicated control deck. The
[layout reference](pocket-arcade-layout.html) preserves its phone dimensions,
square playfield outline and separate Dash and Bank controls. The
[original briefs](prompts.md) record the three alternatives.
